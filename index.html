Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs mobile applications published by MSR Studio on the Google Play Store, including Country Info | দেশ পরিচিতি (com.msr.countryapp), Chhuti (ছুটি) – Bangladesh Travel Guide (com.msr.chhuti), and associated software products.

We are committed to user privacy and practice data minimization. We do not require account sign-ups, passwords, or personal registrations.

2

Information Handling & Storage

We do not collect personal identifiable information (such as personal names, phone numbers, contact books, or financial credentials).

Local Device Storage: In-app user configurations (such as saved favorites, bookmarks, or downloaded offline guide packs) are saved strictly in your device's private sandbox storage (local SQLite / app storage) and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

3

Location Data (GPS) Declaration

Location Usage Summary
Country Info (com.msr.countryapp) does NOT request location access. Location permission is only used by Chhuti (com.msr.chhuti) for calculating distance to nearby attractions.

Location Handling Terms for Chhuti:

  • Ephemeral In-Memory Calculation: GPS coordinates are processed temporarily in memory on your phone to calculate distance to tourist destinations.
  • No Remote Uploads: Coordinates are never stored on or transmitted to external servers.
  • Zero Background Tracking: Location is queried only while you are actively viewing the map or nearby attractions screen in the foreground.
  • Optional: You can revoke location permission at any time in Android settings without losing core app functionality.

Advertising & Google Advertising ID (AD_ID)

Play Console Declaration: Apps contain advertisements
Our free applications display advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support continuous app updates and maintenance.

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may access your Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) to manage ad frequency, prevent fraud, and serve ads.

How to Opt Out: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime by opening Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads) and choosing "Delete advertising ID".

Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Complete User Data Control
Because user data is stored locally on your device, you can completely wipe your data at any time.
  1. Clear App Storage: Go to Android Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Cache to wipe all saved local preferences.
  2. Uninstall: Removing the app permanently erases its local storage sandbox.
  3. Support Email Purge: If you have emailed us for support, you can request email correspondence deletion by contacting blueskyoceanbd@gmail.com.
