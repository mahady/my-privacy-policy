Information We Access & Collect

MSR Studio operates under strict data minimization principles. Neither Country Info nor Chhuti maintains centralized cloud user accounts.

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use in-app features such as saving country facts, bookmarking travel destinations, taking notes, or downloading offline data packages, this information is stored locally in your device's private storage (SQLite database or local cache). It is never sent to our servers.

B. Technical Device Data

When accessing network resources (such as downloading database updates, country flag graphics, or viewing ad impressions), standard technical metadata is processed by Android and third-party SDKs:

  • Device Model & OS Version: To ensure proper layout rendering and compatibility across Android versions;
  • Language & Locale: To automatically switch language between Bengali (বাংলা) and English;
  • Network State: To detect whether an internet connection is active before initiating downloads.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect names, home addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, microphone audio, camera photos, biometric data, or financial/payment credentials.

Location Data (GPS) Declaration • Chhuti Specific

Location Usage Scope: Chhuti (ছুটি) Only • Ephemeral On-Device
Country Info (com.msr.countryapp) does NOT request or use location permissions. Location permissions are only utilized by Chhuti (com.travelguide.bangladesh) for calculating distance to tourist spots.

When using Chhuti, the app may request runtime access to:

  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (approximate location)
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (precise GPS coordinates)

Strict Location Handling Terms:

  1. Ephemeral In-Memory Calculation: Coordinates are processed exclusively on your device in real-time memory to calculate distance to tourist destinations.
  2. No Remote Uploads: Coordinates are never transmitted to MSR Studio servers, third parties, or cloud databases.
  3. Zero Background Tracking: Neither app requests ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. GPS ceases querying as soon as you navigate away from the map or nearby attractions view.
  4. Completely Optional: You can deny location permission; both apps remain functional.

Advertising & Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) Declaration

Play Console Declaration: Yes, our apps contain ads
In compliance with Google Play Store Policies, we declare that both Country Info and Chhuti display in-app advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support ongoing development, research, and server bandwidth.

To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may automatically access your Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and diagnostic signals.

How AdMob Uses Advertising IDs:

  • Frequency Capping: Limiting repetitive impressions of identical ads;
  • Anti-Fraud Protection: Detecting automated clicks, bots, and invalid traffic;
  • Ad Personalization (Subject to Consent): Serving relevant contextual or interest-based ads where permitted.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  3. Select "Delete advertising ID" or toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

For more details, review the Google Advertising Policies.

Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Complete User Autonomy Over Data Deletion
Because MSR Studio apps do not maintain centralized user accounts on remote servers, you can completely erase all your saved data at any moment directly from your phone.

Methods to Clear or Delete Your Data:

  1. Inside the Application: Use the in-app Settings / Offline Manager screen and tap "Clear Cache" or "Reset All Bookmarks".
  2. Via Android Device Settings: Go to Settings > Apps > [Country Info / Chhuti] > Storage & cache > Clear Storage / Clear Cache. This permanently wipes all offline databases, cached images, and user preference files.
  3. Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling the app removes all sandbox storage, offline databases, and cached assets instantly.
  4. Support Correspondence Deletion: If you have emailed us for support or inquiries and wish for your email thread to be purged from our inbox, send a deletion request to blueskyoceanbd@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your emails within 30 business days.

Data Security Measures

We employ robust technical safeguards to protect all data handled across our applications:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All network communications (downloading country data, travel guides, map tiles, and ad assets) utilize encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ protocols.
  • Android Application Sandbox: Local database records and user configurations are sequestered within Android's private app sandbox, preventing unauthorized access by other applications on your device.
  • No Remote User Database: By avoiding unnecessary cloud databases for user activity, we eliminate the risk of remote server database breaches.
